Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to book an appointment in advance?

Yes, we recommend scheduling an appointment to ensure availability, but we also attend emergencies as soon as possible.

Is dental treatment painful?

We use modern techniques and anesthesia to make sure your treatment is as comfortable and painless as possible.

Do you offer orthodontic treatments (braces, aligners)?

Yes, we provide both traditional braces and modern clear aligners depending on each patient’s needs.

Can I whiten my teeth safely?

Yes, we offer professional whitening procedures that are safe and supervised by specialists.

Do you treat children?

Of course, we have experience working with patients of all ages, including children.

How do I know if I need an implant?

If you are missing one or more teeth, implants might be the best option. Our specialists will evaluate your case individually.

How do I take care of my smile after treatment?

We provide personalized post-treatment care instructions to help maintain your results long-term.
